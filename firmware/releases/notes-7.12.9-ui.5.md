# MotoNav universal firmware 7.12.9-ui.5

## New

- **Idle-screen weather on first connect**: the round display now asks the phone for weather (T,W) while on the idle screen too — previously weather was only fetched when you swiped to the weather page, so the idle-page weather slot stayed blank until a manual page visit. After this version, weather appears on the idle screen right after the first BLE connect (phone must have a valid QWeather key configured in the app).

## Unchanged

- Everything else from 7.12.9-ui.4 (pixel second-hand ring, `--:--` clock placeholder, PWR short-press screen off) is unchanged.
